03 · About Biskut

Different flavours,
same tin.

Biskut Inc. is built on a simple idea: local artists should not need to look identical to belong together. Our identity is warm, tactile, playful and proudly rooted in the everyday visual culture around us—from handmade print textures to woven geometric forms and bright tropical colour.

Lokal, bukan literal.We draw from place without turning culture into decoration.
Individual first.Every artist keeps a distinct signature and point of view.
Better together.We pair artists based on what each project actually needs.
Play is serious work.Curiosity, humour and experimentation are part of the process.
